Warm conditional cost plans that exceed the case budget repeatedly rebuild deferred-branch chains. With 128 independent conditions, the runtime fallback creates 8,256 wrappers per evaluation, even when every field is skipped.

This PR reuses links within each runtime evaluation's private deferred chain. Static/assumed-bound traversal, canonical variable order, lookup and floating-point combination order remain unchanged; cached plans and concurrent evaluations share no mutable branch nodes.

### Verdict

**Retained: this runtime-fallback workload improves end-to-end performance.** Across both candidate launches and both surrounding controls, CPU/request falls 18.85–19.75%, median latency 17.04–17.64%, p95 12.25–16.43%, and p99 9.95–15.04%. The microbenchmarks independently show 31,744 B saved at 32 conditions and 520,192 B saved at 128, with less wrapper construction while quadratic scanning remains.

The HTTP improvement is established for the measured 128-condition all-false fallback workload at 200 requests/s. It is not a generic gateway speedup, throughput result or exact default-policy byte claim. Static and ordinary small-condition paths retain their existing microbenchmark limits.
