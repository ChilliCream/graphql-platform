C6/C8 native BenchmarkDotNet decision rubric

Use all seven predeclared launches and all native observations. Verify exact engine/default runtime/MemoryDiagnoser identity, three cases, twelve actual timing observations of1000 completed operations, native diagnostic operation denominator and complete allocation/Gen0/Gen1/Gen2 fields. BDN Result means are elapsed time, not process CPU; retain Actual/overhead, warnings, variance and early/late trends.

For each independent candidate, compare both launches to both adjacent baseline controls and the full baseline range. A repeatable useful elapsed reduction or a reliable absolute allocated-byte reduction can qualify; a speedup is not required when allocation savings are established. Mere absence of regression cannot pass. Repeated material unaffected-path cost, adverse trends or an unstable control envelope can leave net benefit unproven even without a mechanical two-control threshold failure. Record actual magnitudes rather than calling every positive ratio a universal regression.

For asynchronous allocation, retain native MemoryDiagnoser values with their exact operation count and source/runtime scope. Corroborate the proposed change with its measured route and expected allocation object. Surprising zero, inconsistent sign, substantial background variation or lack of cross-thread accounting must remain uncertain. The nine valid baseline and four valid C6 controlled-allocation regions are supporting observations only: they are unmatched partial coverage, not completed proof for C6 and not evidence for unrun C8. Do not subtract arbitrary process noise or invent a tolerance to turn the failed calibration into a pass.

Terminal outcomes: promote only a demonstrated worthwhile net benefit to the next gate; abandon an unproven or adverse candidate with all raw evidence retained. A distinct bounded diagnostic can answer a newly identified validity question only after review and grant. Do not repeat completed timing to seek a favorable result; no virtual-user validation precedes this gate.
